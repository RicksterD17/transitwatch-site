Accessibility

TransitWatch for Android and Wear OS · Version 3.1

TransitWatch is built with the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 in mind. These are the accessibility features it supports, and what each one means in the app.

TalkBack

Every screen works with TalkBack. Each departure is read as one sentence, for example “Millbrae. In 2 minutes, 10 cars; in 22 minutes, 10 cars,” and it says when a time is from the timetable rather than live (“scheduled”) and when a train leaves too soon for you to reach it. The station name is a heading, so you can jump straight to it. Last Ride Home is read as one heading with the time to leave, the walk and the train.

Font size

TransitWatch follows your font size and display size, up to the largest sizes. Text never shrinks to fit: station and destination names wrap onto more lines, and departure times move below the destination. The tab labels at the bottom of the screen stop growing at a size that still fits; TalkBack reads them in full.

Dark theme

TransitWatch follows your phone's dark theme on every screen.

Contrast

All text meets a contrast ratio of at least 4.5:1 in light and dark theme, including gray text such as trains you can't reach and timetable-only times. Line colors stay the same and are not changed by your wallpaper.

Timetable times

Times from the timetable rather than live have a clock icon, so they don't depend on color.

Remove animations

With Remove animations on, switching between walking and driving and other changes happen instantly.

On your watch

TransitWatch on a Wear OS watch works with TalkBack: the station name is a heading, each departure is read as one sentence, and Last Ride Home is read as one heading with the time to leave, the walk and the train. It follows your watch's font size; at the largest sizes, departure times move below the destination instead of shrinking. The watch is always dark, with the same contrast as the phone's dark theme.

Feedback

If something in TransitWatch doesn't work with the way you use your phone or watch, please tell us: transitwatchapp@gmail.com. Settings → Contact Support in the app adds your device details for you.
