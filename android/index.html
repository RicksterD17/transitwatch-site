TransitWatch for Android

The right train departure at a glance, on Android phones and Wear OS watches.

Support

Questions, problems or suggestions? Email transitwatchapp@gmail.com.

If something isn't working, please include your phone model, Android version and the station you were using, and your watch model if it's about the watch. In the phone app, Settings → Contact Support fills in these details for you, including your watch's.

Common questions

Why does a time show a clock icon or say “Scheduled”?
Live predictions weren't available for that train, so TransitWatch is showing the timetable time.

Why doesn't the app find my nearest station?
TransitWatch needs location access while you use it. Check Settings → Apps → TransitWatch → Permissions → Location on your phone (the names vary a little between phone makers), and that your phone's Location is turned on. You can also pick a station from the list.

How are walking and driving times worked out?
TransitWatch estimates them on your phone from the distance to the station, so they can differ a little from a route in a maps app. For turn-by-turn directions, tap a station on the map, then Navigate, which opens Google Maps.

The watch

Does the watch need my phone?
No. TransitWatch on your watch finds the nearest station and shows its departures on its own, over the watch's Wi-Fi or mobile connection or through your phone. Install it from the Play Store on your watch, or from the TransitWatch listing on your phone.

Why doesn't my watch show Last Ride Home?
The watch uses the Home station you choose in TransitWatch on your phone (Settings → Last Ride Home). Your watch gets it the next time it's connected to your phone.

Why is there no Navigate button on my watch?
Navigate opens Google Maps on your watch. If Google Maps isn't installed on the watch, the button doesn't show; you can install Google Maps from the Play Store on your watch.
