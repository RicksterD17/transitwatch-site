Privacy Policy

TransitWatch for iPhone and Apple Watch · Effective November 5, 2026

TransitWatch is built to need as little information about you as possible. There are no accounts, and TransitWatch has no servers of its own.

Location

TransitWatch uses your location while you use the app to find the nearest stations and show their departures. Your location is processed on your device and is not sent to us. The app keeps your last known location on your device for a short time so Siri and widgets can answer quickly.

When you ask for walking or driving times or tap Navigate, your device uses Apple Maps, which handles that request under Apple's privacy policy.

Transit data

TransitWatch downloads timetables and live departure information directly from the transit agencies, or from 511.org, the Bay Area's regional transit data service. Like any internet request, these reach the provider's servers, which can see your device's IP address. The requests do not include your location or any personal information.

Home station, commute settings, Siri and widgets

TransitWatch never asks for your home address. For Last Ride Home, you choose a Home station; the app suggests the stations closest to where you are, and you can pick any station. Your Home station, commute stations and times are stored on your iPhone and Apple Watch only, synced between them, and never sent to us. Widgets and Siri use information saved on your device only.

Anonymous usage statistics

TransitWatch sends at most two small reports a day through TelemetryDeck, a privacy-focused analytics service based in Germany. One report counts which features you used that day (for example, how often you opened the map or a widget), which transit systems you have turned on, and how often live times were available. The other names the one station you viewed most that day and isn't linked to you or to any other report. Reports also include your device type, system version and language settings, as with most apps. The reports never include your location, your Commute or Last Ride settings, or anything that identifies you. You can turn this off in Settings.

Contacting support

When you tap Contact Support in Settings, TransitWatch opens an email draft in your mail app that already includes a few details to help us look into your question: the TransitWatch version, your iPhone model and iOS version, whether TransitWatch has location permission (and whether it's precise), and, if you have an Apple Watch, its TransitWatch and watchOS versions and model. These are shown in the draft, you can change or delete them, and nothing is sent unless you send the email. We use your email only to answer you.

Purchases

If you buy an upgrade, Apple handles the payment. We don't receive your payment details.

Contact

Questions about privacy: transitwatchapp@gmail.com.
