Privacy Policy

TransitWatch for iPhone and Apple Watch · Effective October 30, 2026

TransitWatch is built to need as little information about you as possible. There are no accounts, and TransitWatch has no servers of its own.

Location

TransitWatch uses your location while you use the app to find the nearest stations and show their departures. Your location is processed on your device and is not sent to us. The app keeps your last known location on your device for a short time so Siri and widgets can answer quickly.

When you ask for walking or driving times or tap Navigate, your device uses Apple Maps, which handles that request under Apple's privacy policy.

Transit data

TransitWatch downloads timetables and live departure information directly from the transit agency (currently BART). Like any internet request, these reach the agency's servers, which can see your device's IP address. The requests do not include your location or any personal information.

Home address, commute settings, Siri and widgets

If you set a home address for Last Ride Home, it is stored on your iPhone and Apple Watch only and is never sent to us. When you search for the address, Apple Maps handles the search. Your commute stations and times are also stored on your device and synced between your iPhone and Apple Watch. Widgets and Siri use information saved on your device only.

Usage statistics

TransitWatch may count anonymous events, such as the app being opened, to understand how it's used. These counts never include your location, contacts or any information that identifies you.

Purchases

If you buy an upgrade, Apple handles the payment. We don't receive your payment details.

Contact

Questions about privacy: transitwatchapp@gmail.com.

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
