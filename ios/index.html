Support

Questions, problems or suggestions? Email transitwatchapp@gmail.com.

If something isn't working, please include your iPhone and watchOS versions and the station you were using. In the app, Settings → Contact Support fills in the versions for you.

Common questions

Why does a time show a clock icon or say “Scheduled”?
Live predictions weren't available for that train, so TransitWatch is showing the timetable time.

Why doesn't the app find my nearest station?
TransitWatch needs location access while you use it. Check Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → TransitWatch. You can also pick a station from the list.
