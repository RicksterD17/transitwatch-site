Accessibility

TransitWatch for iPhone and Apple Watch · Version 3.0

TransitWatch is built to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA on iPhone and Apple Watch. These are the accessibility features it supports, and what each one means in the app.

VoiceOver

Every screen works with VoiceOver on iPhone and Apple Watch. Each departure is read as one sentence, for example “Millbrae: in 2 minutes, 10 cars, in 22 minutes,” and it says when a time is from the timetable rather than live (“scheduled”) and when a train leaves too soon for you to reach it. The station name is a heading, so the rotor's Headings setting jumps straight to it. Last Ride Home is read as one heading with the time to leave, the walk and the train.

The map is visual, but its station markers, entrances and buttons can be reached with VoiceOver; the station you're nearest to is always on the Departures screen.

Voice Control

On iPhone, every button and control has a name you can say, such as “Tap Map,” “Tap Show on Map” or “Tap Navigate.”

Larger Text

TransitWatch follows your text size on iPhone and Apple Watch, up to the largest accessibility sizes. At those sizes text never shrinks to fit: station and destination names wrap onto more lines, and departure times move below the destination.

Dark Interface

The iPhone app follows Dark Mode on every screen. The Apple Watch app is always dark.

Sufficient Contrast

All text meets a contrast ratio of at least 4.5:1 in light and dark mode, including gray text such as trains you can't reach and timetable-only times. TransitWatch also follows Increase Contrast.

Differentiate Without Color

Nothing is shown by color alone. Timetable-only times have a clock icon. With Differentiate Without Color on, trains you can't reach in time are also struck through, and Last Ride Home shows an icon as the time to leave gets close, as well as changing color.

Reduced Motion

With Reduce Motion on, switching between walking and driving and other changes happen instantly, without animation.

Siri

You can get departures without looking at the screen. Say “Next train in TransitWatch,” “When's the next train to Millbrae in TransitWatch” or “Last train home in TransitWatch” on iPhone or Apple Watch.

Feedback

If something in TransitWatch doesn't work with the way you use your iPhone or Apple Watch, please tell us: transitwatchapp@gmail.com. Settings → Contact Support in the app adds your device details for you.
